2) Название: Правила сцен (Aztecs)     Референс: нет
----------------------------------------
Follow these rules in every image:
- The story takes place in the Aztec city of Tenochtitlan in central Mexico around 1500 AD, unless the prompt says modern or Europe.
- Aztec people have warm brown skin, straight black hair and normal human faces. Commoners wear plain off-white cloaks of rough plant fiber that end at the knee; women wear loose white blouses and long wrap skirts. Only nobles wear long colorful patterned cloaks, jewelry and feathers.
- Aztec houses are simple white adobe houses with flat roofs. People sleep on woven reed mats on the floor, there are no beds and no chairs in commoners' houses.
- No horses, no metal tools, no wheels, no glass and no European objects in Aztec scenes (except when a SPANIARD is in the prompt).
- Draw exactly the number of people the prompt asks for. "Only one person" means nobody else, no crowd in the background.
- If the prompt says "No people in the scene", draw no people and no human figures at all.
- "Small distant figures" means a wide view with many tiny people, no close-up faces.
- Prompts with "plain warm beige background" are simple object cards: only the objects on a plain beige background.
- A sleeping person always has the eyes fully closed.
- No blood, no violence, no weapons pointed at anyone. Calm, friendly, family-safe scenes.
- Every character is an adult unless the prompt says BOY, GIRL, boy, girl or teenage.
- Make exactly one image per prompt. Never repeat a shot and never make extra variations unless asked.
