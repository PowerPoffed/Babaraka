ОПИСАНИЕ:
What was a normal day like in the Aztec capital? In 1519, Spanish soldiers saw Tenochtitlan and wondered if they were dreaming. Let's follow an ordinary family — from reed mats before sunrise to grinding maize, floating farms, the cleanest streets of the age, a giant market where cacao beans were money, school for every boy, strict rules about cloaks and alcohol, ball games, and the night the world could end.

Coatl and his family are fictional characters, but their day is built from historical sources.
